Signal Tech takes a small number of direct sponsorships aimed at people who ship AI systems, silicon, and developer tools. This page is the media kit. It does not invent an audience size.

Who the desk is for

Reader Tech leads, founders, and engineers following AI engineering, chips, and dev tools. The topic bar also carries security, space, reviews, and community.
Editions Readers can switch region and language. Options include the US, United Kingdom, Germany, France, Japan, India, Brazil, and others. The mix of countries is whatever Google Analytics 4 property G-X1DTQNYD9T shows after a visitor chooses Accept All.
Traffic Quote monthly users and sessions from that GA4 property for the dates you are selling. Analytics does not load before consent, so the property under-counts people who choose Essential Only.
Newsletter The Monday Signal Briefing runs on beehiiv. List size and send stats are available on request from SignalTech.News@gmail.com.

Placements

Empty ad boxes stay collapsed until a real AdSense publisher ID and slot ID replace the placeholders. A direct sponsor can use the same slots. Label paid placements as sponsored. The word “Featured Sponsor” already sits on the sidebar unit.

Sidebar sponsor Desktop only. Hidden below 768px. The column is 340px wide with 16px of padding, so the creative area is about 308px wide and at least 120px tall. It sits above Picks on the home page. Static image, short line, and one link, or a responsive display unit.
In-feed Between the lower story sections on the home page. Full width of the content column, fluid height, at least 120px tall.
Digest banner Inside a weekly digest, before the second section heading. Full width of the article column, at least 90px tall.

Email SignalTech.News@gmail.com is the main way to reach Signal Tech about a sponsorship. Send artwork as PNG or SVG, and the destination URL, with the subject Sponsorship. You can also use the contact page. Include the weeks you want and whether the unit is the sidebar, the in-feed slot, or the digest.

What a sponsor does not get

  • A change to headlines or to which stories the desk runs.
  • A subscriber export from the site itself. Subscriber lists and delivery stats come from beehiiv on request.
  • A guaranteed impression count. Impressions follow the GA4 property and the provider’s send report.
