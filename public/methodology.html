How we select stories

Last updated 11 November 2026

Who picks

Signal Tech is a human-curated aggregation desk for people who build and buy AI systems, chips and developer tools. Innocent Ncube, based in Poland, makes the final selection and writes the commentary. Automation only brings candidates in and applies the pre-filters below.

What we cover

We cover AI engineering, chips and silicon, and developer tools. A news story is kept only when the headline or the short summary is clearly about that work: software and the tools people build with, machine learning, chips, data centres, or software security. Gaming, spaceflight, phones, and general consumer gadgets are not enough on their own. A funding story is kept only when it is also about something a builder would ship.

We skip general AI hype that does not name a system, a chip, or a tool. We also skip pure promotion. Headlines about deals, coupons, discounts, and percent-off offers are dropped before they reach the page. There is no extra filter that tries to guess the tone of a product announcement beyond that.

How a story is ranked

Innocent uses the questions below when deciding what to comment on. They are a human check. They are not hidden weights, and the page does not award points for “shipping impact” or “cost per token” as separate scores.

QuestionWhat we ask
Shipping impactDoes this change what a builder ships this week?
Production relevanceIs it about a system someone can run, not a demo for its own sake?
Silicon and roadmapDoes it move chips, supply, or the next hardware generation?
Cost, latency, reliabilityDoes it change the bill, the wait, or whether the system stays up?
Open and closedDoes it change what you can inspect, run, or take with you?

The automatic sort

News stories also get a simple score that favours recent stories from our core sources and stories with clear builder impact: chips, open tools, and the work of building models. Newer stories rank higher. A “Why it matters” note adds a little, because someone has already marked the story as worth a comment. There are no boosts for particular people or companies.

Papers, reviews, and community posts are not given that score. They are ordered by how new they are, and a story with a picture is preferred when everything else is equal. Stories that look like the same event are grouped, and the strongest one leads. The others stay as related links.

Attribution

  • We do not rewrite a headline to change its meaning. Titles come from the source. Leftover formatting is removed, and a publisher name stuck on the end of a Google News headline is taken off.
  • We do not present aggregated material as original reporting. Every card names the publisher, then the headline, then links to the original source.
  • A “Why it matters” note is Signal Tech’s view, and it is labelled as such. It is not quoted as the publisher’s words.
  • Sponsored items are not filled in yet. When one is added, it will always be labelled “Sponsored”.
